Nodus Presenter 0.2.0 (23) — App Review information

1. QR AND OFFLINE DEMO
Attach nodus-presenter-demo-qr.png. Display it on another screen or print it, launch the app, tap Scan QR code, allow camera access and scan. Its permanent payload, nodus-presenter://demo, opens eight local sample slides and notes. It contains no credentials and does not expire. Alternatively use Try demo, or Paste session link with that URL; those routes require no camera.
The app clearly shows Demo · Not connected to Nodus. Navigation, slide grid, private reading, pointer, Spotlight, drawing, magnifier, timer, black-screen simulation, next preview and layout/appearance settings are available. Commands are simulated locally. Build 23 hides video playback and Mac-volume controls in both the demo presenter and Settings. No computer, account or network is required for the demo. This QR tests scanning and demo entry, not real desktop pairing.

2. REAL DESKTOP PAIRING
Download Nodus Research at https://nodusresearch.com/ (macOS, Windows or Linux). No account or purchase is required for PDF Presenter. Open Toolkit → PDF Presenter, import any sample PDF and select Present. Keep the computer and review device on the same reachable local network, with Wi-Fi enabled on the mobile device and Mac. In the desktop presenter, open Mobile remote (QR) and select iPhone–iPad app (App iPhone–iPad in Spanish), then scan that QR using Nodus Presenter. Allow camera and local-network access. Keep the presentation active; green indicates authenticated state.
The desktop QR contains a temporary key for that local session, so a QR from our computer cannot connect at your location and becomes invalid when its presentation ends. Use the QR generated on your review computer. Real video controls require a slide with video: desktop playback, Mac system volume or Windows/Linux presentation-video volume. External video services require Internet.

3. PHYSICAL-DEVICE VIDEO AND TESTING
The previously supplied Nodus-Presenter-0.2.0-21-iPhone16ProMax.mp4 (3:05) was personally recorded on a physical iPhone 16 Pro Max using build 21. It shows actual QR pairing, slide navigation, pointer/color, Spotlight, drawing, magnifier, timer, next preview, layout/theme and landscape use. It is not a recording of build 23. Build 23 passes 26 iOS session tests and a simulator UI test checking no demo video/volume controls in portrait, landscape and Settings; these are distinct from physical-device validation.

4. PURPOSE, SERVICES AND PRIVACY
A free iPhone/iPad remote for Nodus Research PDF Presenter, available to everyone, including teachers, researchers and students. No mobile account, purchases, subscriptions, advertising, analytics, content feed or messaging. PDFs, notes and drawings are private content exchanged with the paired computer.
Core frameworks: SwiftUI/UIKit, PDFKit, AVFoundation, Network/Bonjour, Multipeer Connectivity and CryptoKit. No mobile developer backend, authentication provider, AI service or third-party analytics SDK. Local PDF/notes/control needs no Internet when devices can communicate. Optional user-selected YouTube overlays play on the desktop.
Privacy: https://nodusresearch.com/presenter/privacy/
Support: https://nodusresearch.com/faq/

5. CONTENT AND REGIONS
This presentation utility is not a regulated service. Bundled demo content and Nodus branding are our own; no licensed media catalog is provided. Users choose their own PDFs/video links. Features are consistent across regions. Languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified/Traditional Chinese; fallback English.
