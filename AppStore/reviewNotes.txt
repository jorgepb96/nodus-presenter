REVIEW WITHOUT A COMPUTER
On the welcome screen, tap Try demo. This local demo works without sign-in, QR pairing or network access and includes eight slides, presenter notes and all remote tools.

Use the arrows or tap the left/right half of the slide to navigate. The grid selects a slide. The book icon enables private reading: navigation then changes only the controller preview, not the audience slide. The moon button blanks/restores the audience screen. The top timer starts/pauses on tap and resets on long press. Settings adjust the slide/notes split, next-slide preview, appearance and nine accent colors. Pointer, Spotlight, drawing and magnifier controls appear between the arrows when active.

Slide 4 demonstrates video play/pause and volume. These controls are visible only for slides containing video and hidden during private reading. Demo playback/volume and other remote commands are simulated; no external computer is controlled in demo mode.

REAL PAIRING
Requires Nodus Research with native Presenter pairing on Mac, Windows or Linux. Download the desktop app from https://nodusresearch.com/. Open a PDF in PDF Presenter, choose the iPhone/iPad app connection and scan its QR with this app. Allow camera access for scanning and local-network access for pairing. A green dot indicates connection.

On Mac the existing direct connection uses Wi-Fi/Bonjour and can operate without the room router depending on devices/environment. Windows and Linux use authenticated TLS on a reachable local network. The app exchanges presentation data only with the computer the user pairs. It has no developer backend or analytics.

Video playback takes place on the desktop audience display. On Mac the volume control adjusts system output; on Windows and Linux it adjusts the presentation video volume.

The app supports English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese; unsupported device languages fall back to English.
