Thank you for reviewing Nodus Presenter. Version 0.2.0 (21):

1. PHYSICAL-DEVICE RECORDING AND QA
Attached: Nodus-Presenter-0.2.0-21-iPhone16ProMax.mp4 (3:05). The developer personally recorded the app on a physical iPhone 16 Pro Max. It begins by opening build 21 from TestFlight, then shows QR pairing with Nodus Research, slide navigation, pointer/color, Spotlight, drawing, magnifier, timer, next-slide preview, layout/theme settings and landscape use. It is the complete recording, re-encoded for attachment compatibility. No registration, login, deletion or paid features exist: no account is created and the app is free. There is no public content feed, user-to-user publishing or messaging. PDFs, notes and drawing are private presentation content exchanged only with the paired computer. Simulator checks cover iPhone/iPad layouts, rotation and languages; they are distinct from physical testing.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A native iPhone/iPad remote for PDF Presenter in Nodus Research. Teachers, researchers, students and other presenters can move around a room while viewing slides/notes and controlling the audience display. It is available to everyone, not restricted to an employer or institution. It has no purchases, subscriptions, advertising or analytics.

3. ACCESS AND SETUP
No credentials are required. Launch the app and tap Try demo (Probar demo in Spanish). Eight included demo slides/notes need no account, computer or network. Arrows or left/right slide taps navigate; the grid selects a slide. The book enables private reading without advancing the audience. The moon blanks/restores the audience. Tap the top timer to start/pause; hold to reset. Settings control the slide/notes split, next preview, appearance and accent. Select pointer, Spotlight, drawing or magnifier; controls appear between the arrows. Slide 4 simulates video play/pause and volume. Video controls appear only on video slides and are hidden during private reading. Demo commands are simulated and do not control a computer.

For real control, download Nodus Research at https://nodusresearch.com/ and open a PDF in PDF Presenter. Start presenting, choose App iPhone-iPad and scan its current QR in the mobile app. Grant camera and local-network access. Keep Wi-Fi enabled and use the same reachable local network for a reproducible review; green indicates authenticated presentation state. Mac QR v1 and Windows/Linux QR v2 remain supported. New Mac Multipeer QR v3 requires the companion desktop integration and is explicitly selected by its QR. Video plays on the desktop audience display; Mac volume controls system output, Windows/Linux volume controls presentation video.

4. EXTERNAL TOOLS AND SERVICES
Core functionality uses Nodus Research desktop and Apple system frameworks: SwiftUI/UIKit, PDFKit, AVFoundation (QR camera), Network/Bonjour, Multipeer Connectivity and CryptoKit. No mobile developer backend, authentication provider, payment processor, AI service or third-party analytics SDK is used. Local PDF/notes/control needs no internet when devices can communicate. Optional user-selected YouTube overlays are played by the desktop and require YouTube/internet; the mobile app sends playback commands. The privacy/support pages open our website: https://nodusresearch.com/presenter/privacy/ and https://nodusresearch.com/faq/.

5. REGIONS
Features and bundled content are consistent across regions. Device-language localization supports English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese and Simplified/Traditional Chinese; other languages fall back to English.

6. REGULATION AND CONTENT
This is a presentation utility, not a regulated service. The bundled demo text/graphics and Nodus branding are our own. It supplies no licensed third-party media catalog. Users select their own PDFs and optional video links; the recording's presentation is a user-selected document, not bundled content.
