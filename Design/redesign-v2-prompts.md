## notes-first

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished native SwiftUI iOS 26 Liquid Glass design proposal for Nodus Presenter, Spanish UI, one tall iPhone portrait screen and one iPad landscape screen side by side on a neutral off-white presentation board. Reference image 2 is the authentic Nodus connected N logo: reuse its exact geometry inside a small monitor/display outline, violet on pale glass, do not invent a letter N. Dock with previous and next chevrons, timer '08:42', play/pause, blackout moon. Compact tool row with pointer, Spotlight sun, drawing pencil and magnifier icons. SF-style symbols, generous hit targets but modest visual footprint. Show full screen contents including bottom safe area. All Spanish typography sharp, no decorative prose, no extra app names. Label proposal tastefully at top of board. Do not suggest user must use same Wi-Fi network. Small next-slide thumbnail in notes header row or below main slide, not a separate section. iPad: slide column left occupies 40 percent width, notes right 60 percent and almost full height. Clean balanced editorial layout. Label board 'A · Notas primero'.
